Why does financial crime occur?

At scale, financial
crime WILL occur
with statistical
regularity if the
right combination
of elements are
present.
TRANSACTIONAL
  • Expectations to meet (financial) targets
  • Hide failing projects or contracts
  • Need for new financing

PERSONAL
  • Financial problems
  • Extravagant life style
  • Addictions (drug or gambling habits)
  • Greed or revenge
  • Character flaws (i.e. sociopathic disorder)

SYSTEMIC WEAKNESSES
  • Lack of oversight
  • Conflict
  • Implementation of new accounting software
  • High volume transactions (easy hiding)
  • New & complex products
  • Grey areas in the rules
  • Ineffective internal controls
