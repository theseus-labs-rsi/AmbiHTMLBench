Security
Detex card for dormitory access and keys for rooms will be issued to all registrants to use during the Festival; if lost there will be a $10. charge for the card and $1. for key.
To Register
In case of cancellation, Festival fee will be refunded less $25.
Complete the registration form with full payment to Sacred Dance Guild Festival and mail to Joan Sparrow, Box 67, South Orleans, Ma. 02662
Extra Information
For additional information contact Joan Sparrow, Registrar, Tel. 617-255-0243 or 617-369-2008 or Gloria Castano, Program Director, Tel. 617-329-4448
Where
The Festival will provide meals and rooms at Endicott College, Beverly, Ma. Class sessions will be there and at nearby Notre Dame Chapel in Ipswich.
Accessibility
By car - Mass Turnpike to Route 128 (North) to Exit 17 (Grapevine Rd.) The sign at the exit reads "Beverly Farms", take a right on to Grapevine Rd. (which becomes Hart St.) and follow to the end. Take a right on Rt. 127 and continue on to Endicott. Pass the main entrance to the college. Further along, registration is at Tupper Hall on the left.

By train - Take Boston and Main Railroad from North Station in Boston to Beverly (Approx. 35 min.) and taxi to Tupper Hall for registration.

By plane - to Logan Airport in Boston, by subway or taxi to North Station, Boston and then by train.
What to Bring
Required - Membership card, a blanket (sheets & pillow case provided) leotard, tights and skirts for dance, casual dress, dance clothes for church service.
Optional - extra towel (only one provided) wash cloth (none provided) flashlight, tennis gear, beach clothes, camera, tape recorder, musical instruments, notebook, alarm clock and wonderful costumes for worship.
Resource Table - photographs, programs, dance books, records and costumes. Please label all items. Not responsible for loss.
Sharing
If you wish to share a solo or group work during prayer times please write information to Program Director giving title, description and equipment needed. Mail to: Gloria Castano, 90 High St. Dedham, Ma. 02026.
