⑿ $d\left(\log_a x\right)=\dfrac{1}{x\ln a}dx$
⒀ $d\left(\arcsin x\right)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx$
⒁ $d\left(\arccos x\right)=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx$
⒂ $d\left(\arctan x\right)=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}dx$
⒃ $d\left(\operatorname{arc}\cot x\right)=-\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}dx$

六、微分运算法则

⑴ $d\left(u\pm v\right)=du\pm dv$
⑵ $d\left(cu\right)=cdu$
⑶ $d\left(uv\right)=vdu+udv$
⑷ $d\left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)=\dfrac{vdu-udv}{v^2}$

七、基本积分公式

⑴ $\int kdx=kx+c$
⑵ $\int x^\mu dx=\dfrac{x^{\mu+1}}{\mu+1}+c$
⑶ $\int\dfrac{dx}{x}=\ln|x|+c$
⑷ $\int a^x dx=\dfrac{a^x}{\ln a}+c$
⑸ $\int e^x dx=e^x+c$
⑹ $\int\cos xdx=\sin x+c$
⑺ $\int\sin xdx=-\cos x+c$
⑻ $\int\dfrac{1}{\cos^2 x}dx=\int\sec^2 xdx=\tan x+c$
⑼ $\int\dfrac{1}{\sin^2 x}=\int\csc^2 xdx=-\cot x+c$
⑽ $\int\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}dx=\arctan x+c$
⑾ $\int\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx=\arcsin x+c$

八、补充积分公式

$\int\tan xdx=-\ln|\cos x|+c$
$\int\cot xdx=\ln|\sin x|+c$
$\int\sec xdx=\ln|\sec x+\tan x|+c$
$\int\csc xdx=\ln|\csc x-\cot x|+c$
$\int\dfrac{1}{a^2+x^2}dx=\dfrac{1}{a}\arctan\dfrac{x}{a}+c$
$\int\dfrac{1}{x^2-a^2}dx=\dfrac{1}{2a}\ln\left|\dfrac{x-a}{x+a}\right|+c$
$\int\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}dx=\arcsin\dfrac{x}{a}+c$
$\int\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2\pm a^2}}dx=\ln\left|x+\sqrt{x^2\pm a^2}\right|+c$

九、下列常用凑微分公式

积分型换元公式
$\int f\left(ax+b\right)dx=\dfrac{1}{a}\int f\left(ax+b\right)d\left(ax+b\right)$$u=ax+b$
$\int f\left(x^\mu\right)x^{\mu-1}d\ =\dfrac{1}{\mu}\int f\left(x^\mu\right)d\left(x^\mu\right)$$u=x^\mu$
$\int f\left(\ln x\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}d\ =\int f\left(\ln x\right)d\left(\ln x\right)$$u=\ln x$
