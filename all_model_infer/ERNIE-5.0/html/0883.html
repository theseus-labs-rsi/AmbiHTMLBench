说明利弊类

第一段：说明现状
1. Nowadays many people prefer XXX because it has a significant role in our daily life.
现在许多人更喜欢XXX，因为它在我们的日常生活中扮演着重要的角色。
2. Generally, its advantages and disadvantages can be seen as follows.
总的来说，它的优缺点如下。
第二段：详细说明利弊
3. First _______ (XXX 的优点之一). Besides _______ (XXX 的优点之二).
第一，……。第二，……。
4. But every coin has two sides. The negative aspects are also apparent.
但是每件事都有两面性，它的缺点也很明显。
5. One of the obvious disadvantages is that _______ (XXX 的第一个缺点). To make matters worse, _______ (XXX 的第二个缺点).
其中一个重要的缺点，……。更糟糕的是，……。
第三段：你对现状（或前景）的看法
6. Through the above analysis, I believe that the positive aspects overweigh the negative ones. Therefore, I would like to _______ (我的选择).
通过以上分析，我认为它的利大于弊。因此，我将会……。
7. From the comparison between these positive and negative effects of XXX, we should deal with it according to the circumstances we are in. Only by this way, _______ (对前景的预测).
通过比较XXX的利弊，我们应该根据我们所处的情况处理此问题。只有通过这种方式……。
