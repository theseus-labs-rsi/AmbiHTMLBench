PERPETRATORS OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PEOPLE OF MALAYSIA AND SINGAPORE

To maintain the interests of British and other foreign capital and the local elites, THEY :

  • SUPPRESS workers' and peasants' rights.
  • BAN independent trade unions and student unions.
  • MANIPULATE judicial process.
  • SUPPRESS parliamentary democracy and press freedom.
