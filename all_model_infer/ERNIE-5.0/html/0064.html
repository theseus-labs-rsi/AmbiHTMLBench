Dear Mr. Ahearn:

As you know, negotiations are getting underway for Chile to join the North American Free Trade Agreement. President Clinton and the other Nafta nations made the invitation last December.

Edelman is handling Chile's communications activities in the United States, and I want to let you know that we can be of assistance when you prepare stories on the upcoming negotiations. We can provide you with a broad range of information on Chile such as media kits and interview opportunities with Chilean officials.

It is important to keep in mind why the Nafta nations selected Chile as their first Latin American partner:

  • Chile's economy has undergone dramatic reform and is one of the most open and advanced in Latin America;
  • Chile can serve as a key building block in expanding economic reforms and more open trade throughout the hemisphere;
  • Chile is a proven investment partner for the U.S.

Please feel free to contact me if I can be of any assistance. My phone number is 202-371-0200.

Sincerely,
