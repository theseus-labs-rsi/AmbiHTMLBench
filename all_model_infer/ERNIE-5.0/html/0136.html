小试牛刀

用be going to /will填空。
  1. ① The sun rise from the east tomorrow.
  2. ② I be careful next time.
  3. ③ Look at the sky. It rain.
  4. ④ Someone is knocking at the door, and I open it.
  5. ⑤ I go to see you if I have time.
参考答案：
①will. 将来事实用will;
② will. 意愿决心用will;
③ is going to. 迹象表明用be going to;
④ am going to. 即将发生用be going to;
⑤ will. 条件句中用will.
