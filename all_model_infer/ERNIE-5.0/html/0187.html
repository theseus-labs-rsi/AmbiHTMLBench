WHAT YOU CAN DO
  • Get the facts and share them with your family and friends
  • Know your sex partners
  • Ask questions about past sexual history and drug use
  • Be honest about your past
If in doubt use a condom (sheath) for sexual intercourse. The AIDS virus cannot get through a condom if it is properly used and does not break. Never share drug needles.
