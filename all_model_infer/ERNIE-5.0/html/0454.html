The all-electric Gold Medallion Home—status symbol of modern living—opens up new vistas for homeowners who want to enjoy the benefits of all-electric living.

Valuable floor space can be utilized with greater flexibility because flameless electric appliances can be placed anywhere . . . without the problems encountered with chimneys, flues and vents.

It has ample Light for Living . . . a lighting system designed for comfort, safety and decorative beauty. And finally, it includes Full Housepower . . . an acceptable ampere service entrance and enough outlets and switches for modern convenience.
