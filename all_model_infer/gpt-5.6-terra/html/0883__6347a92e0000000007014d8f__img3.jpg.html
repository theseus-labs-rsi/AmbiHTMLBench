说明利弊类

第一段：说明现状
1. Nowadays many people prefer XXX because it has a significant role in our daily life.
现在许多人更喜欢 XXX，因为它在我们的日常生活中扮演着重要的角色。
2. Generally, its advantages and disadvantages can be seen as follows.
总的来说，它的优缺点如下。
第二段：详细说明利弊
3. First ______ (XXX 的优点之一). Besides ______ (XXX 的优点之二).
第一，……。第二，……。
4. But every coin has two sides. The negative aspects are also apparent.
但是每件事都有两面性，它的缺点也很明显。
5. One of the obvious disadvantages is that ______ (XXX 的第一个缺点). To make
matters worse, ______ (XXX 的第二个缺点).
其中一个重要的缺点是，……。更糟糕的是，……。
第三段：你对现状（或前景）的看法
6. Through the above analysis, I believe that the positive aspects outweigh the negative
ones. Therefore, I would like to ______（我的选择）.
通过以上分析，我认为它的利大于弊。因此，我将会……。
7. From the comparison between these positive and negative effects of XXX, we should
deal with it according to the circumstances we are in. Only by this way, ______ (对前
景的预测).
通过比较 XXX 的利弊，我们应该根据我们所处的情况处理此问题。只有通过这
种方式……。
