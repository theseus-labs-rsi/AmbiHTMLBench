4) Force Increment Prediction: A Multi-Layer Perceptron, denoted as $\mathrm{MLP}_{\Delta f}$, regresses the high-frequency normal force increment $\Delta \hat{f}_m$ for the current time window derived from the hidden state $h_m$:
$\Delta \hat{f}_m = \mathrm{MLP}_{\Delta f}(h_m) \in \mathbb{R}$ (8)
5) Force Accumulation: The current normal force estimate is updated sequentially. This intermediate prediction relies purely on the integration of the fast-adapting event stream:
$\hat{f}_m = \hat{f}_{m-1} + \Delta \hat{f}_m$ (9)

After iterating through all $M$ packets within the cycle, an accumulated normal force estimate $\hat{f}_M$, driven purely by the transient event stream, is obtained.

d)  Fusion Feature Construction: To counteract integration drift, we concatenate the final accumulated event driven force, $\hat{f}_M$, with the absolute spatial guide frame feature, $\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{frame}}$, constructing a unified multimodal fusion vector, $\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{fusion}}$:
$\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{fusion}} = [\hat{f}_M;\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{frame}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{1+D_f}$ (10)
e)  Correction Term Prediction: The fusion feature $\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{fusion}}$ is processed by calibration network ($\mathrm{MLP}_{\mathrm{corr}}$) to predict a 1D force correction term, $\Delta f_{\mathrm{corr}}$. This component explicitly learns to compensate for accumulated drift by anchoring the estimate to the reliable absolute spatial deformation captured by the image frame:
$\Delta f_{\mathrm{corr}} = \mathrm{MLP}_{\mathrm{corr}}(\mathbf{f}_{\mathrm{fusion}}) \in \mathbb{R}$ (11)
