7-2-1. FDC NOTAM
EXPIRATION/CANCELLATION

a. The FDC NOTAM originator is responsible for
canceling FDC NOTAM prior to end of validity;
otherwise, the NOTAM cancellation is
automatically processed.

b. When a new FDC NOTAM is issued to correct
or in any way change a previously issued FDC
NOTAM, a new NOTAM will be issued, and a
separate cancellation NOTAM will be issued to
cancel the old NOTAM.
