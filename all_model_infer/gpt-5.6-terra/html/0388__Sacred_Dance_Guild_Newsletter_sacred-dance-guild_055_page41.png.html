Security

Delete cards for dormitory access and keys for rooms will be issued to all registrants to use during
the Festival; if lost there will be a $10. charge for the card and $1. for keys.

In case of cancellation, Festival fee will be refunded less $25.

To Register

Complete the registration form with full payment to Sacred Dance Guild Festival and mail to
Joan Sparrows, Box 67, South Orleans, Ma. 02662

Extra Information

For additional information contact Joan Sparrows, Registrar, Tel. 617-255-0243 or 617-369-2008 or
Gloria Castano, Program Director, Tel. 617-329-4448

Where

The Festival will provide meals and rooms at Endicott College, Beverly, Ma. (Class sessions will be
there and at nearby Notre Dame Chapel in Ipswich.

Accessibility

By car - Mass Turnpike to Route 128 (North) to Exit 17 (Grapevine Rd.) The sign at the exit reads
“Beverly Farms”, take a right on to Grapevine Rd. (which becomes Hart St.) and follow to the end.
Take a right on Rt. 127 and continue on to Endicott. Pass the main entrance to the college.
Further along registration is at Tupper Hall on the left.

By train- Take Boston and Main Railroad from North Station in Boston to Beverly (Approx. 35 min.)
and taxi to Tupper Hall for registration.

By plane - to Logan Airport in Boston by subway or taxi to North Station, Boston and then by train.

What to Bring

Required - Membership card, a blanket (sheets & pillow case provided) leotards, tights and skirts
for dance, casual dress, dance clothes for church services.

Optional - extra towel (only one provided) wash cloth (none provided), flashlights, tennis gear,
beach clothes, camera, tape recorder, musical instruments, notebooks, alarm clock and wonderful
costumes for worship.

Resource Table - photographs, programs, dance books, records and costumes.   Please label all items.
