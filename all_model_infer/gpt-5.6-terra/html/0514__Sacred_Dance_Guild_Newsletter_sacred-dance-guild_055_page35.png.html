Sacred Dance Guild 20th Anniversary Festival
Endicott College Beverly, Mass.
June 21-25, 1978
The Variety and The Unity of Spiritual Gifts....I Corinthians 12:4-7

"There is a variety of gifts but always the same Spirit; there are all sorts of service
to be done, but always to the same Lord; working in all sorts of different ways in different
people, it is the same God who is working in all of them......"
Through the years the Sacred Dance Guild has offered leadership in a variety of dance
forms and religious denominations and with this 20th anniversary year we continue to
build on the foundation of both traditional and contemporary expression. With this
and in coming Festivals the culmination of the conference will be in the creating,
participation and experience of a particular denominational worship service and its
time in history.

Artists In Leadership
Dance

The Spirit Moves - A Workshop In Dance and Prayer - Through the interaction of self,
improvisation and prayer the workshop will explore a variety of ways dance and prayer
can enhance liturgy and illuminate scripture varying with the feasts and seasons in
the church year allowing us to become more involved in the word of God as it touches us.

Carla DeSola, a graduate of the Juilliard School of Music, has danced and given workshops
on dance and prayer from California to New York, for colleges, retreat centers, parishes
and religious conferences. She has produced for television, writes a monthly column on
dance for Liturgy and is the author of Learning Through Dance and The Spirit Moves ....
a handbook on dance and prayer. Presently she is teaching at the College of New Rochelle,
New York Theological Seminary and directing the Omega Liturgical Dance Company of New
York City.

Music

Calliope Consort - Music for Worship and Dance - Music from biblical times through
the 17th century will be played on medieval, renaissance, baroque and folk instru-
ments such as violas da gamba, recorders, krummhorns, pipe and tabor, percussion,
harp, rauschefeiffe, psaltry and cornamuse.

The Calliope Consort is directed by Nancy Nichols and assisted by Sybille Volz and members
of the Consort. The group has performed at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston; The Fogg
Museum, Cambridge; the chapel at the Mass. General Hospital; before the Middlesex Health
Association; at lecture demonstrations and for many schools and libraries. In addition
to numerous church services, worship and wedding celebrations of a variety of denomina-
tions, they have participated in Shakespeare's Twelfth Night as well as their own original
creation.
