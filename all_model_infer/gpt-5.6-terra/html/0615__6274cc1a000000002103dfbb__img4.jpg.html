此外，社会、教育和文化促使强化某些关于孤独的非理性信念。

Society has a tendency to think that if someone is 40 years
old and living alone, there's something wrong.

社会有一种倾向，如果一个人40岁了，仍在独自生活，那就有
问题了。

However, it should be realized that the central problem tends
to be the belief surrounding it.

然而，应该认识到，核心问题往往是围绕它的信念。

In other words, the problem is not loneliness itself, it's what's
said about it, how it's interpreted , and the meaning it's
given.

换句话说，问题不在于孤独本身，而在于人们对它说了什么，
如何解释它，以及它被赋予的意义。

Today, we live in crowded places and are incredibly
connected to each other, thanks to social media.

如今，多亏了社交媒体，我们生活在拥挤的地方，彼此之间有
着难以置信的联系。

Nevertheless, it may be that this isn't enough for you, and
you feel alone because you're single or live completely
alone.

然而，这对你来说可能还不够，你会感到孤独，因为你单身或
者完全独居。

However, as we mentioned earlier, that isn't the problem.

然而，正如我们之前提到的，这不是问题。

It's your way of seeing it and the dialogue you're having with
yourself about it that's the issue.

问题在于你看待它的方式和你自己的对话。

That's what makes you feel anxious, depressed, and empty
inside.

这就是让你感到焦虑、沮丧和内心空虚的原因。

Obviously, if you tell yourself a hundred times a day or more,
that being alone is a terrible thing, that nobody loves you, and
you'll end up dying alone, your emotions will be extremely
intense and you'll feel dreadful because of these ideas and
thoughts that don't even correspond with reality.

显然，如果你每天告诉自己一百次或更多次，孤独是一件可怕
的事情，没有人爱你，你最终会孤独地死去，你的情绪会非常
强烈，你会感到可怕，因为这些想法和思想甚至与现实不符。
