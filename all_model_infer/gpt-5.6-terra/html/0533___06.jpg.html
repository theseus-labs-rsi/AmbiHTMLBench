SAS OFFICER COMMITTED SUICIDE AFTER BEING SUSPECTED OF KILLING

ANOTHER MENTAL DISORDER OR CONSCIENCE DISCOVERY?

Simon Riley, an officer of the Royal Air Force Special Air Service (SAS), was wanted by police for a serious killing some time ago.

Simon Riley, an officer of the Royal Air Force Special Air Service (SAS), was wanted by police for a serious killing some time ago.

It is reported that on the night of Christmas, which should be full of joy, Riley brutally killed his mother, his brother's family and the psychologist who consulted them. This series of violent crimes quickly aroused strong public indignation and anxiety.

[Photo: indistinct scene]

Investigators revealed that Riley may have had serious mental problems before committing the crime, which may be one of the important reasons that led him to the extreme road. However, although people around him may have noticed his abnormality, they failed to take effective measures in time to stop the tragedy.

隶属于英国皇家空军特种空勤团(SAS)的军官 Simon Riley，前段时间因为涉嫌一起严重的杀戮事件而被警方通缉。

据报道，在圣诞节这个本应充满欢乐的夜晚，Riley残忍地杀害了自己的母亲、弟弟一家以及为其进行心理咨询的心理医生。这一连串的暴力行为迅速引发了公众的强烈愤慨和不安。

调查人员透露，Riley在作案前可能已存在严重的精神问题，这或许是导致他走上极端道路的重要原因之一。

[Photo: burning building / police scene]
INTENTIONAL
ARSON?

After the incident, Riley once disappeared and the investigation was deadlocked.

Then his whereabouts finally fell. Unfortunately, when people found his trail, he had committed suicide in his own house.

What is even more shocking is that before committing suicide, Riley deliberately set fire to the whole house in an attempt to destroy all the evidence that might leave clues.

Because of the fierce fire, Riley's body was seriously damaged when rescuers arrived at the scene. Nevertheless, the police and forensic doctors found a barely recognizable military identification card in the ruins through careful search, thus confirming that the identity of the deceased was Simon Riley, who had been wanted for many days.

更令人震惊的是，在自杀前，Riley还故意纵火，将整个住宅付之一炬，企图销毁所有可能留下线索的证据。

由于火势猛烈，Riley的尸体在救援人员赶到现场时已被严重损毁。尽管如此，警方和法医仍然通过仔细搜寻，在废墟中找到了一块勉强可辨认的军用识别牌，从而确认了死者的身份正是通缉多日的Simon Riley。这一发现无疑为案件的侦破工作画上了句号，但同时也留下诸多未解之谜。

This discovery undoubtedly put an end to the investigation of the case, but it also left many unsolved mysteries.

For example, whether the body in the burning mansion is really Riley, and why did he kill his family? But there is no doubt that this case has come to an end in the open.

例如，起火的宅邸里究竟是否是Riley，他又为何会杀害自己的家人。但无疑本案在明面上已经尘埃落定。

There was a murder in Christie Cancer Hospital, and a man was killed in the hospital. It is understood that the time of the incident was around night, and the staff and patients in the hospital were busy with their own affairs. When checking the ward, one of the nurses found that the man in a certain bed had died.

After receiving the report, the police immediately organized the police to rush to the scene to investigate. Through preliminary investigation, the police found that the crime scene did not leave too many traces. It seems that the patient did not have the strength to fight back, so it was impossible to locate more information about the murderer.

In addition, the police also revealed that the victim had no obvious dispute or conflict with others before being killed, thus ruling out the possibility of revenge killings caused by personal grievances.

克里斯蒂肿瘤医院发生了一起凶杀案件。一名男子在该医院内被杀害。据了解，案发时间大约在夜间，医院内的工作人员和病人都在忙着自己的事情。在检查病房时，其中一名护士发现某张病床上的男子已经死亡。

警方接到报案后，立即组织警力赶赴现场展开调查。通过初步勘查，警方发现案发现场并未留下太多痕迹，似乎该名病人并没有反抗的能力，因此无法定位更多有关凶手的信息。

此外，警方还透露，受害者在遇害前并未与他人发生明显的争执或冲突，因此排除了因个人恩怨引发报复性杀人的可能性。
