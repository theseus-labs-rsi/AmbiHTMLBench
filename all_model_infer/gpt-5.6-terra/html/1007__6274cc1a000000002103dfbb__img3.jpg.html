Ask yourself what loneliness means to you.

问问自己孤独对你意味着什么。

If you're telling yourself that loneliness is a terrible thing, that

it's dangerous to be alone, and that you can't fend for yourself in

life, you have a problem.

如果你告诉自己孤独是一件可怕的事情，孤独是危险的，你不

能在生活中照顾自己，你就有问题了。

Therefore, you must change these irrational ideas for more

rational and realistic ones.

所以，你一定要把这些非理性的想法，换成更理性、更现实的

想法。

Spend time with you

与您共度时光

Force yourself to spend time alone.

强迫自己独处。

In order to change, you must not only act on a mental or

cognitive level, but also on a behavioral level.

为了改变，你不仅要有精神或认知层面上的意识，还要在行为

层面上行动。

Program entire weekends alone with your own company.

计划整个周末单独由自己陪着自己。

Enjoy a book or a movie if you want but avoid social media.

如果你愿意，享受一本书或一部电影，但避免社交媒体。

Go to the movies, to the beach, to the mountains, alone.

去看电影，去海滩，去山上，一个人。

Take a trip without any company.

在没有任何陪伴的情况下进行一次旅行。

When you do this for long enough, you'll realize that you

survived, that your fears weren't real, and that you've met

your own true self and now you know yourself better.

当你这样做足够长的时间后，你会意识到你幸存了下来，你的

恐惧不是真的，你已经遇到了真正的自己，现在你更了解自己

了。

Connect with others

与他人联系

Manage your time so you surround yourself with people.
