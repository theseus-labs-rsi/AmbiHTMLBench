c.It is unclear whether pre-vaccine nivolumab contributed to vaccine-
induced T cell responses:
Major pathologic responses were noted post-vaccination melanoma
patients who did not initially respond to nivolumab.
Refining the sequence of different agents in combination immunotherapy for best clinical outcomes needs further study.
Alternative immuno-oncologic agents, such as anti-CTLA-4, that can preferentially
expand naïve CD8+ T cells to increase the T cell pool for vaccine priming, might be
better a choice for pre-vaccine immunotherapy.
