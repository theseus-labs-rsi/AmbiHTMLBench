$1-\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{3}{20}-\dfrac{3}{20}$
$=1-\dfrac{5}{20}-\dfrac{3}{20}-\dfrac{6}{20}$
$=\dfrac{20}{20}-\dfrac{5}{20}-\dfrac{3}{20}-\dfrac{6}{20}$
$=\dfrac{6}{20}=\dfrac{3}{10}$
