Why climate changes
  1. Changes in Earth's orbit (Milankovitch cycles)
    • Eccentricity (~100,000 year cycle) – how elliptical Earth's orbit is
    • Obliquity (~41,000 year cycle) – how tilted the Earth's axis is
    • Precession (~23,000 year cycle) – position of equinoxes over time
