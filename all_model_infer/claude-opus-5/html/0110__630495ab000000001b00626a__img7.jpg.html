Limitations

b.  Approximately 25% of enrolled patients were unable to proceed to vaccination:

•   The majority due to either disease progression or inability to generate vaccines because of low tumor content in the biopsy samples or a low number of mutations.

→ Opportunities to mitigate this issue include:

  1. Acceleration of vaccine production timelines
  2. Incorporation of potentially higher-yield tumor sampling approaches
  3. Selection of alternative clinical settings can be envisioned

Limitations

c.  It is unclear whether pre-vaccine nivolumab contributed to vaccine-induced T cell responses:

•   Major pathologic responses were noted post-vaccination melanoma patients who did not initially respond to nivolumab.

→ Refining the sequence of different agents in combination immunotherapy for best clinical outcomes needs further study.

✓  Alternative immunoncologic agents, such as anti-CTLA-4, that can preferentially expand naive CD8+ T cells to increase the T cell pool for vaccine priming, might be better a choice for pre-vaccine immunotherapy.
