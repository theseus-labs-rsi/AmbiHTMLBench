本系列面料使用裘皮与针织加入少量彩色进行设计，将不同品种的裘皮进行拼接染色等工艺再造。或者将裘皮与其他面料进行拼接嫁接产生、特别的效果，再其次将裘皮进行不同颜色的拼接拼出不同肌理的感觉，黑牛仔面料则采用拼接穿绳。抽丝做出肌理感在加入印花系列标志整个系列更加突出主题，并且使用裘皮契合会大赛要求。
This series of fabrics is designed by adding a small amount of black and white jeans to fur and knitting. Different kinds of fur are reconstructed by splicing and dyeing, or the fur is spliced and wound with other fabrics.Special effect, secondly, fur is spliced with different colors to give different feeling of texture, black denim fabric is interspersed with splicing, silk drawing to make texture feeling in addition to the printing series logo to make the wholeSeries more prominent theme, and the use of fur match the requirements of the competition.
