2Q Comments
  • Sales down 2% due to lower volumes resulting from the scheduled ethylene outage and portfolio impact of the GLS/Vinyls sale
  • Demand for Performance Polymers up mid-single digits due to strengthening automotive market
  • Demand environment in consumer, construction, and food packaging markets remains stable
  • Operating earnings down 9% as higher sales in automotive were more than offset by reduced supply of ethylene and the portfolio impact of the GLS/Vinyls sale
3Q Outlook
  • 3Q sales essentially flat due to the GLS/Vinyls sale
  • Consumer, construction, and food packaging volumes forecasted to remain stable
  • Automotive volumes anticipated to remain strong led by China and Europe
  • 3Q operating earnings up modestly versus prior year which included a $30 million benefit related to a JV equity position
