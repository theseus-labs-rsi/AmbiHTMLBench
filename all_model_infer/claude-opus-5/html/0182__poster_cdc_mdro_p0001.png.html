Use EBP during high-contact care activities for residents with:

  1. 1 Indwelling Medical Devices
    (e.g., central line, urinary catheter,
    feeding tube, tracheostomy/ventilator)
  2. 2 Wounds
  3. 3 Colonization or Infection
    with a MDRO
