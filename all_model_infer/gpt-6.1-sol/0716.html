A Note on Second-Order Expected Maximum-Load Bounds for
Binary Linear Hashing

Nader H. Bshouty
Technion

Abstract

Let $S\subseteq\mathbb{F}_2^u$ have size $n=2^\ell$, and let $h:\mathbb{F}_2^u\to\mathbb{F}_2^\ell$ be a uniformly random linear map. For $y\in\mathbb{F}_2^\ell$, write $\operatorname{load}_h(y):=|h^{-1}(y)\cap S|$, and let $\mathrm{M}(S,h):=\max_{y\in\mathbb{F}_2^\ell}\operatorname{load}_h(y)$ be the maximum load. Jaber, Kumar and Zuckerman (STOC 2025) proved that the expected maximum load of $h$ on $S$ is at most $16\log n/\log\log n$, matching the fully independent keys-into-bins setup to constants. Their proof also gives the tail estimate

$$\Pr\left[\mathrm{M}(S,h)\ge R\frac{\log n}{\log\log n}\right]\le O\left(\frac{1}{R^2}\right).$$

We record a base optimization in their exponential-potential method showing that binary linear hashing nearly matches fully independent hashing also at the level of the second-order maximum-load scale. For every $R>1$ satisfying $R\ell^{1-1/R}\ge D\ln\ell$, where $D$ is an absolute constant, we prove

$$\Pr\left[\mathrm{M}(S,h)\ge R\frac{\log n}{\log\log n}\right]\le O\left(\frac{(\log\log n)^2}{R^2(\log n)^{2-2/R}}\right).$$

Integrating this tail yields

$$\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{M}(S,h)]\le\left(1+(1+o(1))\frac{\log\log\log n}{\log\log n}\right)\frac{\log n}{\log\log n}.$$

Thus binary linear hashing matches fully independent hashing in the leading term and matches the dominant second-order correction up to a $1+o(1)$ factor.

We also prove, by an independent self-contained argument, a sharp tail bound for one prescribed bucket: for fixed $y\in\mathbb{F}_2^\ell$,

$$\Pr[\operatorname{load}_h(y)>2^a-2]\le\gamma^{-1}2^{-a^2},$$

where $\gamma=\prod_{j\ge1}(1-2^{-j})$. A subspace construction shows that this is asymptotically tight even in the leading constant as $a\to\infty$. However, this controls only a fixed bucket; a direct union bound over all buckets loses a factor $2^\ell$.
