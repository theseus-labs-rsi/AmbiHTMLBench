The Market Fundamentals

The healthcare market is fundamentally different from
any other commodity market. The fundamental difference
is one cannot predict when demand will rise and fall.
COVID-19 did away with all underlying presumptions
about how the market behaves.

If patients are customers, the customers themselves cannot
plan ahead on when to opt for healthcare services. ER/
Accident distinguish this market in fundamental ways.

Healthcare Coverage Across the US

Healthcare coverage has increased in America over the years
due to the following reasons.

  • Government-sponsored Medicaid plans
  • Medicare
  • Liberalization of laws
  • Competition amongst private health insurers
  • State-level health plans
