$\displaystyle \int f(e^x)\cdot e^x\,dx = \int f(e^x)\,d(e^x)$ $u = e^x$
$\displaystyle \int f(a^x)\cdot a^x\,dx = \frac{1}{\ln a}\int f(a^x)\,d(a^x)$ $u = a^x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\sin x)\cdot \cos x\,dx = \int f(\sin x)\,d(\sin x)$ $u = \sin x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\cos x)\cdot \sin x\,dx = -\int f(\cos x)\,d(\cos x)$ $u = \cos x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\tan x)\cdot \sec^2 x\,dx = \int f(\tan x)\,d(\tan x)$ $u = \tan x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\cot x)\cdot \csc^2 x\,dx = \int f(\cot x)\,d(\cot x)$ $u = \cot x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\arctan x)\cdot \frac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx = \int f(\arctan x)\,d(\arctan x)$ $u = \arctan x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\arcsin x)\cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx = \int f(\arcsin x)\,d(\arcsin x)$ $u = \arcsin x$
十、分部积分法公式
(1) 形如 $\displaystyle \int x^n e^{ax}\,dx$，令 $u = x^n$，$dv = e^{ax}dx$
形如 $\displaystyle \int x^n \sin x\,dx$ 令 $u = x^n$，$dv = \sin x\,dx$
形如 $\displaystyle \int x^n \cos x\,dx$ 令 $u = x^n$，$dv = \cos x\,dx$
(2) 形如 $\displaystyle \int x^n \arctan x\,dx$，令 $u = \arctan x$，$dv = x^n dx$
形如 $\displaystyle \int x^n \ln x\,dx$，令 $u = \ln x$，$dv = x^n dx$
(3) 形如 $\displaystyle \int e^{ax}\sin x\,dx$，$\displaystyle \int e^{ax}\cos x\,dx$ 令 $u = e^{ax}$，$\sin x$，$\cos x$ 均可。
十一、第二换元积分法中的三角换元公式
(1) $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$   $x = a\sin t$   (2) $\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}$   $x = a\tan t$   (3) $\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}$   $x = a\sec t$
【特殊角的三角函数值】
(1) $\sin 0 = 0$ (2) $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ (3) $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (4) $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$ (5) $\sin\pi = 0$
(1) $\cos 0 = 1$ (2) $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (3) $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ (4) $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 0$ (5) $\cos\pi = -1$
