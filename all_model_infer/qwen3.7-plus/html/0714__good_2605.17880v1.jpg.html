Thrall's Problem for Two Rows
Jisun Huh, Woo-Seok Jung, Jang Soo Kim, and Meesue Yoo
Abstract. In this paper, we study Thrall's problem for the higher Lie modules \(L_\lambda\). Our main result provides a tableau-theoretic description of the Schur expansion of the character of \(L_\lambda\) when \(\lambda\) has two rows, thereby solving Thrall's problem in this case. This formula is expressed in terms of standard Young tableaux with major index congruence conditions and a spin-parity condition defined through bijections with Yamanouchi domino tableaux. We also obtain tableau formulas for hook shapes and partitions with distinct parts, and these results extend to all partitions in which each part greater than 2 occurs at most twice.
1. Introduction

In this paper, we study Thrall's problem for the higher Lie modules. We begin by describing the problem. See Section 2 for the undefined terms.

Consider the \(\mathbb{C}\)-vector space \(V = \mathbb{C}^N\) and let \(T(V) = \bigoplus_{n \ge 0} V^{\otimes n}\) be the tensor algebra on \(V\), graded by tensor degree with \(V\) in degree 1. The free Lie algebra \(\mathrm{Lie}(V)\) on \(V\) is the Lie subalgebra of \(T(V)\) generated by \(V\) under the commutator bracket \([x, y] = xy - yx\). Since the bracket is homogeneous with respect to the tensor grading, we have \(\mathrm{Lie}(V) = \bigoplus_{m \ge 1} \mathcal{L}_m(V)\), where \(\mathcal{L}_m(V) := \mathrm{Lie}(V) \cap V^{\otimes m}\) is the homogeneous component of degree \(m\). For a partition \(\lambda\), the higher Lie module \(\mathcal{L}_\lambda(V)\) indexed by \(\lambda\) is defined by

\[ \mathcal{L}_\lambda(V) := \bigotimes_{i \ge 1} \mathrm{Sym}^{m_i(\lambda)}(\mathcal{L}_i(V)), \tag{1.1} \]

where \(m_i(\lambda)\) denotes the number of parts equal to \(i\) in \(\lambda\). We also use the convention that \(\mathcal{L}_\emptyset(V)\) denotes the trivial one-dimensional \(\mathrm{GL}(V)\)-module.

By the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem, the tensor algebra is identified as a graded \(\mathrm{GL}(V)\)-module with the symmetric algebra on the free Lie algebra:

\[ T(V) \cong \mathrm{Sym}(\mathrm{Lie}(V)) \cong \bigoplus_{\lambda} \mathcal{L}_\lambda(V), \]

where the direct sum is over all partitions \(\lambda\). Thus the higher Lie modules arise naturally from the isomorphism above. For brevity, we write

\[ L_\lambda := \mathcal{L}_\lambda(V), \qquad L_n := L_{(n)}. \]

For \(g \in \mathrm{GL}(V)\), let \(x_1, \ldots, x_N\) be the eigenvalues of \(g\). Then the character \(\mathrm{ch}(L_\lambda)\) of the polynomial \(\mathrm{GL}(V)\)-module \(L_\lambda\) is a symmetric polynomial in \(x_1, \ldots, x_N\). In 1942, Thrall [8] asked for the Schur decomposition of the higher Lie modules \(L_\lambda\). Equivalently, the problem can be stated as follows.

Problem 1.1 (Thrall's Problem). For a partition \(\lambda\) of \(n\), find a combinatorial interpretation for the Schur coefficients of the characters \(\mathrm{ch}(L_\lambda)\) in the limit \(N \to \infty\):
\[ \mathrm{ch}(L_\lambda) = \sum_{\mu \vdash n} c_{\lambda,\mu} s_\mu. \tag{1.2} \]
