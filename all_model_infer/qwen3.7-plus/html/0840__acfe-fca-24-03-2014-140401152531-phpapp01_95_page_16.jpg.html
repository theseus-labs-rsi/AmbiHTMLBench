Fraud risk assessment

Why does financial crime occur?

At scale, financial crime WILL occur with statistical regularity if the right combination of elements are present.

TRANSACTIONAL

  • Expectations to meet (financial) targets
  • Hide failing projects or contracts
  • Need for new financing

PERSONAL

  • Financial problems
  • Extravagant life style
  • Addictions (drug or gambling habits)
  • Greed or revenge
  • Character flaws (i.e. sociopathic disorder)

FRACTURED LOGIC...

  • "My performance isn't recognized"
  • "Management doesn't care"
  • "Everyone else is does it"
  • "I'm only borrowing"
  • "It's the cost of doing business"
  • "I have been mistreated and deserve compensation"

SYSTEMIC WEAKNESSES

  • Lack of oversight
  • Conflict
  • Implementation of new accounting software
  • High volume transactions (easy hiding)
  • New & complex products
  • Grey areas in the rules
  • Ineffective internal controls
