c.
It is unclear whether pre-vaccine nivolumab contributed to vaccine-
induced T cell responses:
  • Major pathologic responses were noted post-vaccination melanoma
    patients who did not initially respond to nivolumab.
→ Refining the sequence of different agents in combination
immunotherapy for best clinical outcomes needs further study.
  • Alternative immunoencologic agents, such as anti-CTLA-4, that can preferentially
    expand naive CD8+ T cells to increase the T cell pool for vaccine priming, might be
    better a choice for pre-vaccine immunotherapy.
