Elsevier wants to control Open Data

Speaking on the 28th of April 2014 the Vice
Chancellor of Cambridge University, Leszek
Borysiewicz, commented on the amount the
university is paying to publisher Elsevier. He said:

Yes we spend money with Elsevier. Do I regret
spending money with Elsevier? By and large
yes I do because I think they’re rich enough
already.

[asked by Michelle Brook]

and:

just wait until we get into open data debates ... Elsevier is already looking at ways in which it can control open
data as a private company rather than the public bodies concerned.
