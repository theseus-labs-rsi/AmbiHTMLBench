PERPETRATORS OF CRIMES AGAINST THE
PEOPLE OF MALAYSIA AND SINGAPORE

To maintain the interests of British and other foreign capital
and the local elites, THEY

  • SUPPRESS workers' and peasants' rights.
  • BAN independent trade unions and student unions.
  • MANIPULATE judicial process.
  • SUPPRESS parliamentary democracy and press freedom.

THEY JAIL AND KILL PEOPLE

THE BRITISH CONNECTION

RUBBER AND TIN from colonial Malaya were crucial to British
post-war recovery.

[Photograph: worker tapping a rubber tree]

"Without Malaya, the Sterling System, as we know
it could not exist."  Purcell

A 12-year British Military Rule was imposed after
the World War II to protect British economic interests.

Repressive laws were first introduced by the British
colonial regime, then further improved by the local
elites.

[Photograph: armed men and a bicycle]

Though formally 'independent', Britain CONTINUES to exert influence
and control through

  • Domination of the economies
  • Collaboration with the repressive regimes
  • Cultural subversion
  • Five-Power-Defence-Arrangement (U.K., Australia, New Zealand,
    Malaysia and Singapore).
