WHAT IS SOCIAL
DISTANCING?

Social distancing, also called physical distancing, is a
form of non-pharmaceutical intervention, which
downtrends the spread of contagious diseases simply
by keeping people “apart” from each other.

[Exclamation mark icon]

WHY IS SOCIAL
DISTANCING IMPORTANT?

It prevents contact between infected and uninfected
individuals, resulting in reduced disease transmission
and less deaths from fatal diseases.

[Check mark icon]

BEST PRACTICES FOR
SOCIAL DISTANCING

[House icon]

Self/Home Quarantine: Stay home. Only go out
if it’s very essential, that too with protection
(gloves, masks, sanitizers)

[Group of people icon]

Avoid public gatherings as much as possible

[Storefront icon]

Keep spots susceptible to mass gatherings
like parks, malls, food courts, restaurants
and schools closed. Restaurants can be
instructed to follow a “Takeaway” only
system until the situation relaxes

[Measuring tape icon]

Maintain a distance of at least 1 - 2 meters
(3 to 6 feet) from other individuals

[Handshake icon]

Avoid shaking hands and embracing one another

[Line chart icon]

SOCIAL DISTANCING IN THE TIME
OF COVID-19: FLATTENING THE CURVE

For a disease like COVID-19, which spreads through air droplets from sneezing and coughing, scientists have claimed
that social distancing might “flatten the curve”.

This means that if proper social distancing protocols are followed, the spread of the disease will slow down and
relieve health care professionals and medical centers of the burden of providing aid to hundreds of patients at a
time, which is not feasible.
