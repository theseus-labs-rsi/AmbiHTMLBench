Jalapeno Bread

1 lb. Loaf
Ingredients1 lb.
Water (80°F – 90°F)½ cup
Oil1 ½ tbsp.
Canned Whole Kernel Corn,
well drained		½ cup
Jalapeno Peppers, well
drained		2 TBL
Sugar¾ tsp.
Salt¼ tsp.
Bread Flour2 cups
Bread Machine Yeast¾ tsp.
Corn Meal⅓ cup
Fresh Cilantro, chopped2 tsp.
Active Dry Yeast1 ¾  tsp.

DIRECTIONS

  • Place ingredients in the pan of
    the bread machine in the order
    the ingredients are listed.
  • Insert bread pan securely into
    baking chamber; close lid. Plug
    unit into wall outlet.
  • Select white bread cycle, crust
    color, and the correct  loaf size;
    press Start.
  • When the bread is finished
    and all cycles are complete,
    remove the pan from the
    chamber (using potholders).
  • Remove the bread from the
    pan and allow to cool (at least
    20 minutes).
