[Pale green floral divider]
  1. ①will. 将来事实用will;
  2. ② will. 意愿决心用will;
  3. ③ is going to . 迹象表明用be going to ;
  4. ④ am going to . 即将发生用be going to ;
  5. ⑤ will. 条件句中用will.
