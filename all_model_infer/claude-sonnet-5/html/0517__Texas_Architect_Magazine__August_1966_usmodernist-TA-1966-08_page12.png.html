MR. RICHARD IVES, DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT, WASHINGTON, D.C., GAVE THE FOLLOWING REMARKS:

  • I can think of no better way of expressing my feeling at this conference than to point to the charter of El Paso of 1960. This is what the U.S. Government will have to use as a guide to work out a realistic program.
  • We are indeed very fortunate in having a distinguished group of professionals to identify this problem, and responsible local officials to work jointly to create a better environment for economic and social development. These are the basic objectives of this kind of activity.
  • The Chamizal project is a special effort and sets an example to follow. It was made possible principally because of the work of the International Boundary and Water Commission.
