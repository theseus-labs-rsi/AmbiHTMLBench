In reference to the memorandum regarding the implementation of the Most Essential Learning Competencies (MELCs) for School Year 2024–2025, all public elementary and secondary schools in the Division of Cebu Province are hereby directed to ensure the faithful and effective delivery of the identified competencies across all learning areas.

This Office emphasizes the following:

  1. All teachers shall orient their learners and parents/guardians on the MELCs and the expected learning outcomes for the school year.
  2. School heads shall monitor the implementation of the MELCs through regular classroom observations and review of lesson plans and instructional materials.
  3. Teachers are encouraged to contextualize and localize the MELCs to make them relevant and responsive to the needs of their learners.
  4. Quarterly assessments shall be conducted to measure learner progress and mastery of the essential competencies.
  5. Reports on the implementation of the MELCs shall be submitted to this Office not later than [illegible] of each quarter.

For guidance and compliance. For any queries or clarifications, please contact the Curriculum Implementation Division (CID) at [illegible].

Immediate dissemination of this memorandum is desired.
