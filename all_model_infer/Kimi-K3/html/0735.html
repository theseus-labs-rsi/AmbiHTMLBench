如何看待线上教育?
  • Introduction 开头
    • The pandemic is ranging across the globe, and students are reeling from it.
      (背景) During the lockdown, students have no other choice but to study from home, making online education more popular. (引出话题) In my view, it has both pros and cons, and should be used wisely. (抛出观点)
  • Pros 好处
    • For students, online education is more flexible. Rather than being restricted in classrooms, leaners can sign up for any course they are interested in as long as they have the access to the Internet. (对于学生，课程选择更加灵活)
    • For teachers, they don't need to make the roll call, because the system can record automatically. Besides, they can also assign, collect and review the homework more conveniently. (对于老师，教学更加便捷)
  • Cons 不足
    • Online courses may be effective for self-directed leaners, but students who are not organized and in possession of good time-management skills may struggle. (对于自驱型学习者有效，反之，效果不佳)
    • Online education is lacking in teacher-student interaction. It's more difficult for teachers to ask feedbacks from students. And students also can't ask questions immediately. (师生间缺少互动)
  • Conclusion 结论
    • Therefore, students should ask themselves whether they are capable of learning independently before signing up for online coursework. (所以在参与线上课程之前，学生首先应该对自己的自主学习能力有所判断)
