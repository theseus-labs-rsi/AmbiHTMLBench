(2) 求$\frac{a^2+b^2}{c^2}$的最小值.

【解析】$\frac{\sin 2B}{1+\cos 2B}=\frac{2\sin B\cos B}{2\cos^2 B}=\frac{\sin B}{\cos B}=\frac{\cos A}{1+\sin A}$

$\Rightarrow \sin B+\sin B\sin A=\cos B\cos A$

$\Rightarrow \sin B=\cos B\cos A-\sin B\sin A=\cos(B+A)=-\cos C$

$\because B,C\in(0,\pi)$, $\therefore \sin B>0\Rightarrow \cos C<0$, $C>\frac{\pi}{2}$, $B\in(0,\frac{\pi}{2})$,

$\sin B=\sin(C-\frac{\pi}{2})\Rightarrow B=C-\frac{\pi}{2}$或$B+C-\frac{\pi}{2}=\pi$(舍)

(1) $B=C-\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{6}$

(2) 因为我们有角的关系,我们将边化为角: $A=\pi-B-C=\frac{\pi}{2}-2B$; $C=\frac{\pi}{2}+B$

由正弦定理: $\frac{a^2+b^2}{c^2}=\frac{\sin^2 A+\sin^2 B}{\sin^2 C}=\frac{\cos^2 2B+\sin^2 B}{\cos^2 B}=\frac{(2\cos^2 B-1)^2+1-\cos^2 B}{\cos^2 B}$

令$x=\cos^2 B$, $x\in(0,1)$

$\frac{a^2+b^2}{c^2}=\frac{(2x-1)^2+1-x}{x}=\frac{4x^2-5x+2}{x}=4x+\frac{2}{x}-5\geqslant 2\sqrt{8}-5=4\sqrt{2}-5$

$4x=\frac{2}{x}\Rightarrow x=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$时取等号
