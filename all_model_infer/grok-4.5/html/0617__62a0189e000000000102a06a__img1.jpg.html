【解析】$\dfrac{\sin 2B}{1+\cos 2B}=\dfrac{2\sin B\cos B}{2\cos^{2}B}=\dfrac{\sin B}{\cos B}=\dfrac{\cos A}{1+\sin A}$

$\Rightarrow\sin B+\sin B\sin A=\cos B\cos A$

$\Rightarrow\sin B=\cos B\cos A-\sin B\sin A=\cos(B+A)=-\cos C$

∵$B,C\in(0,\pi)$，∴$\sin B>0\Rightarrow\cos C<0$，∴$C>\dfrac{\pi}{2}$，$B\in(0,\dfrac{\pi}{2})$，

$\sin B=\sin(\pi-C)\Rightarrow B=C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$或$B=\pi-C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-C$（舍）

（1）$B=C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$

令$x=\cos^{2}B$，$x\in(0,1)$

$\dfrac{a^{2}+b^{2}}{c^{2}}=\dfrac{(2x-1)^{2}+1-x}{x}=\dfrac{4x^{2}-5x+2}{x}=4x+\dfrac{2}{x}-5\geqslant2\sqrt{8}-5=4\sqrt{2}-5$
