REPORT ON THE STATE OF THE UNION
Delivered by the President
Date: January 20, 2025
Introduction
Ladies and gentlemen, good evening. Tonight I stand before you to report on the state of our Union — a Union that is stronger, safer, and more united than ever before.
The past year has been one of extraordinary achievement. We have protected our borders, created millions of new jobs, and restored common sense to our government.
Economic Highlights
Unemployment has reached a historic low of 3.1%.
Inflation is under control at 2.4%.
Wages are rising faster than prices.
The stock market has set new records.
