Framing Cameras 🇩🇪
By imaging the surfaces of Vesta and Ceres, the framing cameras will enable scientists to map the topography, albedo and color, and to determine their shapes, volumes, and spin rates.

In its journey to the main asteroid belt to orbit two diverse protoplanets, Vesta and Ceres, Dawn is carrying a suite of sophisticated scientific instruments to collect data that will inform us about the early formation of our solar system.
Gravity Science 🇺🇸
Gravity science: the spacecraft transmits a radio signal to Earth through its high gain antenna that changes in frequency as the spacecraft's speed changes when flying over areas of different densities. The collective set of information on the mass variations or concentrations is called the gravitational field of the body/planet.

Visible & Infrared Spectrometer 🇮🇹
VIR measures reflected sunlight and emitted thermal radiation from the surfaces of the targets. Scientists extract and then map mineralogical signatures and thermal properties. These properties hold clues to the history and formation mechanisms of Vesta and Ceres.
