Section 1: Overview

The core priorities of any high-fidelity OCR-to-HTML conversion are as follows:

  1. Fidelity of Content — Extract all visible text verbatim with zero paraphrasing or summarization.

  3. Visual Styling — Match fonts, sizes, spacing, and borders to approximate the original appearance as closely as possible.

Section 2: Hard Constraints
