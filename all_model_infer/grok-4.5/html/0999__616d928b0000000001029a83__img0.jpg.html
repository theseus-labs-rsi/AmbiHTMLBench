Opening Remarks

Good morning, distinguished attendees. Today we convene to address the rising incidence of cardiovascular complications linked to prolonged screen time. The statistics are alarming: over 68% of American adults spend more than eight hours daily on digital devices.

Key Fact: A recent study found that individuals averaging six or more hours of screen exposure per day show a 32% higher risk of developing hypertension compared to those under four hours.

Conclusions & Recommendations

1. Implement daily screen-time caps of no more than four hours for adults.
2. Promote 10-minute movement breaks every hour.
3. Expand public awareness campaigns through digital and traditional media.
