This CODE shall be known as the BRUSHES AND BROOMS CODE for the purposes of the National Industrial Recovery Act.

To effectuate the policies of the said Act the following provisions are established as a Code of Fair Competition for the Brush and Broom Industry under Title I of said Act.

ARTICLE I - PURPOSES

To promote the general welfare by promoting the policies set forth in Section 3 of the Act.

To cooperate with other industries in the preservation of the economic stability of the nation.

To promote the use of brushes and brooms by the public.

To promote the use of the trade-mark "Brush and Broom" by the public.

ARTICLE II - DEFINITIONS

The term "Industry" as used herein includes, but without limitation, the following:
(1) The manufacture of brushes and brooms for household and industrial use.
(2) The manufacture of brushes and brooms for household and industrial use.
(3) The manufacture of brushes and brooms for household and industrial use.

ARTICLE III - TRADE PRACTICE PROVISIONS

Section 1 - Price

It is an unfair trade practice for any member of the Industry to sell or offer to sell any brush or broom at less than the minimum price fixed by the Trade Practice Committee.

Section 2 - Credits

It is an unfair trade practice for any member of the Industry to sell or offer to sell any brush or broom on credit terms more liberal than those fixed by the Trade Practice Committee.

Section 3 - Advertising

It is an unfair trade practice for any member of the Industry to use any advertisement or promotion that misrepresents the quality or performance of any brush or broom.

Section 4 - Combination

It is an unfair trade practice for any member of the Industry to combine with any other person or firm for the purpose of fixing prices or otherwise restraining trade.

ARTICLE IV - TRADE ASSOCIATION

Every member of the Industry shall become a member of the Brush and Broom Trade Association (hereinafter called the "Association") as a condition of his right to conduct business under this Code.

The Association shall submit to the President of the United States (hereafter called the "President") an itemized budget of its estimated expenditures for the first three months of operation and for each three-month period thereafter. The President may disapprove any budget or part thereof if in his judgment the expenditures are excessive or not in the public interest.

After the first three months of operation the Association may submit to the President a revised budget of its estimated expenditures for the next three months, and the President may disapprove any part thereof.

ARTICLE V - TRADE PRACTICE COMMITTEE

The Trade Practice Committee shall consist of three (3) members appointed by the President from nominations submitted by the Association.

The Trade Practice Committee shall have the power to fix minimum prices, credit terms, and advertising standards.

The decisions of the Trade Practice Committee shall be final and binding on all members of the Industry.

ARTICLE VI - COMPLIANCE

Every member of the Industry shall comply with this Code and with all regulations promulgated thereunder.

The President shall have the power to enforce this Code and to punish violations.
