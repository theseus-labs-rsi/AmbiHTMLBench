The Power of Kindness
A Short Story by Martin
August 2, 2026

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Martin. Martin lived in a small town with his family. He always helped his neighbors and shared his toys with his friends. One day, Martin found a lost puppy in the park. He brought the puppy home and named it Buddy. Martin took very good care of Buddy. He fed him every day and walked him in the park.

Q1: Where did Martin find the puppy?
A1: In the park.

Q2: What did Martin name the puppy?
A2: Buddy.

Q3: How did Martin take care of Buddy?
A3: He fed him every day and walked him in the park.

Q1: Who was Martin? Martin was a kind boy.
Q2: What did Martin do with the puppy? Martin brought the puppy home.

One day, a big storm came and the river rose. Martin ran to help people who were in danger. He saved many lives. After the flood, Martin and his family helped the people in the town. They gave food and clothes to everyone.

Q3: What happened one day? A big storm came and the river rose.

Q4: How did Martin help after the flood?
A4: He and his family gave food and clothes to everyone.

Now Martin is a teacher. He teaches children to be kind. He says, "Be kind to others and you will be happy."
