Social DISTANCING

WHAT IS SOCIAL DISTANCING?

Social distancing, also called physical distancing, is a form of non-pharmaceutical intervention, which downtrends the spread of contagious diseases simply by keeping people “apart” from each other.

WHY IS SOCIAL DISTANCING IMPORTANT?

It prevents contact between infected and uninfected individuals, resulting in reduced disease transmission and less deaths from fatal diseases.

BEST PRACTICES FOR SOCIAL DISTANCING

Self/Home Quarantine: Stay home. Only go out if it’s very essential, that too with protection (gloves, masks, sanitizers)
Avoid public gatherings as much as possible
Keep spots susceptible to mass gatherings like parks, malls, food courts, restaurants and schools closed. Restaurants can be instructed to follow a “Takeaway” only system until the situation relaxes
Maintain a distance of at least 1 - 2 meters (3 to 6 feet) from other individuals
Avoid shaking hands and embracing one another

SOCIAL DISTANCING IN THE TIME OF COVID-19: FLATTENING THE CURVE

For a disease like COVID-19, which spreads through air droplets from sneezing and coughing, scientists have claimed that social distancing might “flatten the curve”.

This means that if proper social distancing protocols are followed, the spread of the disease will slow down and relieve health care professionals and medical centers of the burden of providing aid to hundreds of patients at a time, which is not feasible.
