THE POISSON DISTRIBUTION

Suppose that the number of occurrences of some event in a specified interval...

$$P(X = x) = \frac{e^{-\lambda} \lambda^x}{x!}$$

where $x$ is a non-negative integer.

The mean and variance of the Poisson distribution are both equal to $\lambda$.

Theorem 1.1

If $X$ is a Poisson$(\lambda)$ random variable, then its moment generating function is...

Proof:

$$E[e^{tX}] = \sum_{x=0}^{\infty} e^{tx} \frac{e^{-\lambda} \lambda^x}{x!} = e^{-\lambda} \sum_{x=0}^{\infty} \frac{(\lambda e^{t})^x}{x!} = e^{-\lambda} e^{\lambda e^{t}} = e^{\lambda(e^{t} - 1)} \ldots$$
