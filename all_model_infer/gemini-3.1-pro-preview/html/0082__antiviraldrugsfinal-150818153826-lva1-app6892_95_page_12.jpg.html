Cidofovir

❖ Uses:
  • CMV-induced retinitis in patients with AIDS
❖ Adverse effects:
  • Dose related kidney damage
  • Neutropenia, metabolic acidosis, uveitis and ocular hypotony also occur
  • Note: Probenecid must be coadministered with cidofovir to reduce the risk of nephrotoxicity, but probenecid itself causes rash, headache, fever, and nausea
❖ Contraindications:
  • Patients with preexisting renal impairment
  • Patients taking concurrent nephrotoxic drugs, including NSAIDS
