PERPETRATORS OF CRIMES AGAINST THE
PEOPLE OF MALAYSIA AND SINGAPORE
To maintain the interests of British and other foreign capital and the local elites, THEY
: SUPPRESS workers' and peasants' rights.
: BAN independent trade unions and student unions.
: MANIPULATE judicial process.
: SUPPRESS parliamentary democracy and press freedom.
THEY JAIL AND KILL PEOPLE
THE BRITISH CONNECTION
RUBBER AND TIN from colonial Malaya were crucial to British post-war recovery.
[Photo: Plantation worker / rubber tapper at tree]
"Without Malaya, the Sterling System, as we know it could not exist." Purcell
A 12-year British Military Rule was imposed after the World War II to protect British economic interests.
Repressive laws were first introduced by the British colonial regime, then further improved by the local elites.
[Photo: British soldiers on patrol interrogating local on bicycle]
Though formally 'independent', Britain CONTINUES to exert influence and control through
- Domination of the economies
- Collaboration with the repressive regimes
- Cultural subversion
- Five-Power-Defence-Arrangement (U.K., Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia and Singapore).
BELIEVED TO BE IN LONDON ATTENDING
