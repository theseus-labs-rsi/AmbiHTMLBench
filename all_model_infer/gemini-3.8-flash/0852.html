Abstract
We study approximation by shallow $\text{ReLU}^s$ networks, $\sigma_s(t) = \max\{0, t\}^s$, and the generalization behavior of such networks under $\ell_1$ path-norm control. For the $L^p$-type integral spaces $\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}_{p,\tau_d,s}$, $1 \le p \le 2$, we prove approximation bounds for shallow networks using spherical harmonic analysis. In particular, when the parameter measure is the uniform measure $\tau_d$ and $p < p^* = \frac{2d+2}{d+3}$, we obtain the rate $O\Big(m^{-\frac{1}{2} - \frac{d(2-p)}{2d(2-p)+2p(2s+d+1)}} \log^{\frac{3}{2}} m\Big)$, which improves the corresponding random-feature rate. We also derive approximation rates for Sobolev spaces $W^{\alpha,p}$ in the range $1 \le p < 2$ by embedding them into spectral Barron spaces. Finally, for nonparametric regression with sub-Gaussian noise, we prove minimax-optimal generalization bounds for path-norm-regularized shallow $\text{ReLU}^s$ networks: the rates are $O\Big(n^{-\frac{d+2s+1}{2d+2s+1}} \log n\Big)$ over $\mathscr{B}_s$ and $O\Big(n^{-\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+d}} \log n\Big)$ over $W^{\alpha,\infty}$, with matching lower bounds up to logarithmic factors.
Keywords: Neural Networks, Sobolev Spaces, Variation Spaces, Path Norm

1 Introduction

Deep learning has shown remarkable effectiveness in high-dimensional approximation problems, particularly in scientific computing, inverse problems, and operator learning (Han et al., 2018; Adcock et al., 2022; Beck et al., 2023). In many such settings, the $\text{ReLU}^s$ activation $\sigma_s(t) = \max\{0, t\}^s$ ($s \in \mathbb{N}_0$) is especially relevant because it yields piecewise-polynomial representations that are well suited to smooth targets and derivative-sensitive tasks (Yang and Zhou, 2025; He et al., 2024).
