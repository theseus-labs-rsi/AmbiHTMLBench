六(2)班
Protect
the Earth
Smoke from cars makes
the air dirty.
Too much plastic is bad
for the Earth.
[Illustration: Cars driving on road emitting thick grey exhaust smoke]
[Illustration: Green meadow littered with discarded plastic bottles & bags]
[Illustration: City park with bench, tree, and rubbish scattered on ground]
[Illustration: Deforested landscape with chopped tree stumps against starry sky]
Rubbish makes the
park messy and dirty.
Trees help keep the
air clean.
We should not cut down
too many trees.
Let's protect our Earth!
1. We should not waste water.
2. We should reuse and save water.
3. There is not much coal or oil on Earth.
We should save energy
4. We should use paper bags and glass bottles.
5. We should put rubbish in the bin.
