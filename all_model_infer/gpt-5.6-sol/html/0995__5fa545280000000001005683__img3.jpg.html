② $d\left(\log_a x\right)=\dfrac{1}{x\ln a}\,dx$

⑤ $d\left(\arctan x\right)=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx$

（1）$\int kdx=kx+c$

（5）$\int e^x dx=e^x+c$
（6）$\int\cos xdx=\sin x+c$

（8）$\int\dfrac{1}{\cos^2x}dx=\int\sec^2x\,dx=\tan x+c$
（9）$\int\dfrac{1}{\sin^2x}dx=\int\csc^2x\,dx=-\cot x+c$
