Judith Rock - board of directors: SDG; M. Div. San Francisco Theological Seminary; M.A.
in dance, Mills College; ordained, United Presbyterian Church; Visiting Scholar in Dance
in Religion, Graduate Theological Union, Berkeley 1977; involved in free-lance ministry
in dance and theology; author of Theology in the Shape of Dance.

The Choreographer As Creator   Exploring the process of developing "word, image or idea"
into dance. Participants should come with a list (mental and/or written) of some of the
significant things in their inner landscapes: titles, words, dreams, jokes, relatives,
colors, shapes, gestures, sounds etc. It will be helpful but not necessary to have read
Theology in the Shape of Dance.

Group III

Gregory Mitchell - graduate of the Juilliard School, president of the Juilliard Christian
Fellowship; performed solo work under Jose Limon, Anna Sokolow and Alvin Ailey as well
as ensemble work with Rudolf Nureyev in "Sleeping Beauty", National Ballet of Canada;
danced principal roles for the American Opera Center and [illegible] Opera and has toured England
and Holland with "Shekinah" a company of Christian artists as both dancer and choreographer.
Most recently he is touring with the Eliot Feld Ballet in addition to extensive performance
in churches and Christian events both here and abroad.

Creative Praise - Craft and Prospective   Through exercises in trust, theater games and
improvisations, the session will explore motivations and desires, work through "idea
and approach" and seek to incorporate a sense of form, fusing mind and spirit.

Linda Kahn Seaton - member of the SDG; attended Southern Illinois University and graduated
with a B.F.A. in Dance from University of Illinois; has an extensive performance background
as choreographer, dancer and singer in productions "The Little Prince", "What Child is
This", "Four Saints in Three Acts", "Jesus Christ Superstar" and many others performed
in concerts, schools and churches throughout the mid-west. Teaching credits include
lecture demonstrations on: The Elements and Organization of Dance; Dance as a Form of
Worship; Exploration of Worship Through the Arts. Currently, movement instructor,
choreographer and division coordinator for Athens Children's Theater, Athens, Ohio.

Scriptural Choreography - Probing new Approaches for Moving   The group will interpret
selected passages from the Psalms in sign language and then integrate those signed
passages into a choreographed piece expressing biblical truths. The dance will be
fully expressive as God's word is manifested in time, space and energy through manual
and body movement.
