小试牛刀

用be going to /will填空。
①
The sun rise from the east tomorrow.
②
I be careful next time.
③
Look at the sky. It rain.
④
Someone is knocking at the door, and I open it.
⑤
I go to see you if I have time.
参考答案：
  1. ①will，将来事实用will;
  2. ②will，意愿决心用will;
  3. ③is going to，迹象表明用be going to;
  4. ④am going to，即将发生用be going to;
  5. ⑤will，条件句中用will.
