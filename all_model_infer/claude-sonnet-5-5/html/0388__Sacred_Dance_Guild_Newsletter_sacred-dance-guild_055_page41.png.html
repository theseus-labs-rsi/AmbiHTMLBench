Where

The Festival will provide meals and rooms at Endicott College, Beverly, Ma. Class sessions will be

there and at nearby Notre Dame Chapel in Ipswich.

Accessibility

By car - Mass Turnpike to Route 128 (North) to Exit 17 (Grapevine Rd.) The sign at the exit reads

"Beverly Farms", take a right on to Route 128 (North) to Grapevine Rd. (which becomes Hart St.) and follow to the end.

Take a right on Rt. 127 and continue on to Endicott. Pass the main entrance to the college.

Further along, registration is at Tupper Hall on the left.


By train- Take Boston and Main Railroad from North Station in Boston to Beverly (Approx 35 min.)

and taxi to Tupper Hall for registration.


By plane - to Logan Airport in Boston, by subway or taxi to North Station, Boston and then by train.

What to Bring

Required - Membership card, a blanket (sheets & pillow case provided) leotard, tights and skirts

for dance, casual dress, dance clothes for church service.

Optional - extra towel (only one provided) wash cloth (none provided) flashlight, tennis gear,

beach clothes, camera, tape recorder, musical instruments, notebook, alarm clock and wonderful

costumes for worship.

Resource Table - photographs, programs, dance books, records and costumes. Please label all items.

Not responsible for loss.
