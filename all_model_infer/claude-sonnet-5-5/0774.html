地域文化视域下旅游文创产品设计研究
郭继业1,2
(1.内蒙古师范大学　国际设计艺术学院，呼和浩特　010020；
2.设计与社会创新内蒙古高校人文社科重点研究基地，呼和浩特　010020)

摘要： 目的 探索旅游文创产品在地域文化的滋养下所进化的各种设计形式与创新方法。 方法 从民俗的 地域文化一直植根于民族的历史传承发展者，是中的精华元素推了文化艺术设计领域以可带的经验和全新 的见述。本文正是基于这一现实发展背景，以文化创意设计的应用与发展视角出发，提出将地域文化元 素与旅游文创产品设计的融合可性，涉及的相关合价的重要价值为依据，深入总结旅游文创产品设计中的 挖掘元素、找准定位、丰富表彩等不思路，探讨了文化、功能、情感等层面的有效应用与创新。 结论 旅 游文化视域下旅游文创产品设计应以丰富的内涵和文化隐性迭合为当代人的审美观，并以人民日常生活为 地域文化的主要参考，突出设计者的独特创意，构建与产品、文化的完美结合体，以丰富而独特的旅 游文创产品去探寻好的全新发展。

关键词：地域文化；文创产品；创意

Design of Tourism Cultural and Creative Products from the
Perspective of Regional Culture
GUO Ji-ye1,2
(1.International Design Art College, Inner Mongolia Normal University, Hohhot 010020, China;
2.Design and Social Innovation Key Research Base of Humanities and Social Sciences of Inner
Mongolia Universities, Hohhot 010020, China)

ABSTRACT: The work aims to explore various design forms and innovative methods of tourism cultural and creative products under the nourishment of regional culture. The regional culture of China has been inherited and developed with the pace of the times, the essence of which has given valuable experience and new inspiration to the field of culture, art and design. Based on the reality of this development background, the possibility of integrating regional culture elements into design of tourism cultural and creative products was put forward from the application and development perspective of product design. With the combination of these two important values as the basis, the design ideas of tourism cultural and creative products, such as exploring elements, determining audiences and enriching categories, were put forward. The ef- fective application and innovation in levels such as culture, function and emotion were discussed. Design of tourism cul- tural and creative products from the perspective of regional culture should cater to the modern aesthetics with rich con- notation and cultural attributes. In addition, it should also highlight the unique creativity of designers and construct a perfect combination of people, products and culture with people's daily life and regional culture as the main reference, so as to achieve good social effect with rich and unique tourism cultural and creative products.

KEY WORDS: regional culture; cultural and creative products; originality
