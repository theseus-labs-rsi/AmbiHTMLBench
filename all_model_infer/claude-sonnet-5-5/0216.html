顾译文
六(2)班
Protect
the Earth
Smoke from cars makes
the air dirty.
Too much plastic is bad
for the Earth.
[Illustration: Circle divided into 4 quadrants — top-left: grey road with two cars and smoke; top-right: green hillside with scattered plastic trash (bags, bottles, shoe); bottom-left: light-blue park scene with tree, bench, and litter on ground; bottom-right: dark night sky over hills with tree stumps (deforestation)]
Rubbish makes the
park messy and dirty.
Trees help keep the
air clean.
We should not cut down
too many trees.
Let's protect our Earth!
1. We should not waste water.
2. We should reuse and save water.
3. There is not much coal or oil on Earth.
We should save energy
4. We should use paper bags and glass bottles.
5. We should put rubbish in the bin.
[Illustration: Heart-shaped Earth (green continents on blue ocean) with a wall-mounted faucet dripping water into a puddle below]
