While fire-safety ranks first among the advantages of Kalman Steel Joists, they bring additional values that appeal to any prospective home-builder.

The combination of Kalman Steel Joists with concrete floor slab and plaster makes a floor structure that, while forming an impassable barrier to fire, transmits very little sound and vibration, preventing noises overhead from disturbing occupants of rooms below. A floor structure that cannot shrink to cause cracks where walls and floor meet, and that is immune to the dreaded termite, whose ravages are spreading rapidly.

These advantages, which make a difference in the building cost of only a few cents a square foot, provide the owner with a dwelling that is more

Kalman Steel Joists in no way complicate the builder's work. Often they simplify it. These joists require no cutting or fitting. They reach the site in the exact lengths needed and are quickly installed and the concrete poured.
