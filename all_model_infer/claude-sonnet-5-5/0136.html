✏️ 用be going to /will填空。
① The sunrise from the east tomorrow .
② I be careful next time .
③ Look at the sky. It rain.
④ Someone is knocking at the door, and I open it.
⑤ I go to see you if I have time.
📝 参考答案:
①will，将来事实用will；
② will，意愿决心用will；
③ is going to ，迹象表明用be going to ；
④ am going to ，即将发生用be going to ；
⑤ will，条件句中用will。
