STRIKES
A SCHOOL OF WAR,
BUT NOT THE WAR ITSELF.
A strike teaches workers to understand what the strength of the employers and what the strength of the workers consists in; it teaches them not to think of their own employer alone and not of their own immediate workmates alone but of all the employers, the whole class of capitalists and the whole class of workers.
[Photo: Black-and-white photograph of a large crowd of workers confronting a line of police officers wearing helmets, at a street demonstration. A red banner with a white ribbon/scroll design and a circular inset photo of an industrial building is held up on the right side of the image, partially overlapping the crowd. Some people are seen climbing on top of a vehicle (bus) in the upper left background.]
A strike, moreover, opens the eyes of the workers to the nature, not only of the capitalists, but of the government and the laws as well.
LENIN
