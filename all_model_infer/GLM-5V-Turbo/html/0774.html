地域文化视域下旅游文创产品设计研究

郭继业1,2
(1.内蒙古师范大学 国际设计艺术学院，呼和浩特 010020；
2.设计与社会创新内蒙古自治区高校人文社科重点研究基地，呼和浩特 010020)
摘要：[目的]探索旅游文创产品在地域文化的传承下所迸发的各种设计形式与创新方法。[方法]我国的地域文化在垂直时代的影响下继承发展来看，其中的精华充斥了文化与艺术设计领域以可视的视觉整合的美感。本文正是基于这一客观发展需要，从文创到产品的应用与发展角度出发，提出将地域文化元素与旅游文创产品的融合的必要，并以此阐释结合的重要价值内涵，深入挖掘旅游文创产品进行中的轮廓要素、形状层次、丰富意蕴等思路，除了文化、功能、情感、品质等内在的有效点与原创、跨界、地域文化化赋予下旅游文创产品设计应以深度的画面和个性化塑造结合带来的美感，并以人民日常需求与地域文化化为主要参考，突出设计的独特特质，构架人与产品、文化与人的完整融合。
关键词：地域文化；文创产品；原创性

Design of Tourism Cultural and Creative Products from the
Perspective of Regional Culture
GUO,Ji-ye1,2
(1.International Design Art College, Inner Mongolia Normal University, Hohhot 010020, China;
2.Design and Social Innovation Key Research Base of Humanities and Social Sciences of Inner
Mongolia Universities, Hohhot 010020, China)
ABSTRACT: The aim is to explore various design forms and innovative methods of tourism cultural and creative products under the nourishment of regional culture. The regional culture of China has been inherited and developed with the passage of times, the essence of which has given valuable experience and new inspiration in the field of culture, art and design. Based on the reality of this development background, the possibility of integrating regional culture elements into design of tourism and creative products was put forward with the application and development perspective of creative design. With the combination of these two important values as the basis, the design ideas of tourism cultural and creative products, such as exploring elements, determining audiences and enriching categories were put forward. The effective application and innovation from levels of culture, function and emotion were discussed. Design of tourism cultural and creative products in the perspective of regional culture should cater to the modern aesthetics with rich connotation and cultural attributes addition, if it should be daily life aesthetic designers and construct a perfect integration between people and products, culture and people.
KEY WORDS: regional culture; cultural and creative products; originality
