Abstract
We study approximation by shallow ReLU* networks, $\sigma_{s}(t)=\max\{0,t\}^{s}$, and the generalization behavior of such networks under $\ell_{1}$ path-norm control. For the $L^{p}$-type integral spaces $\mathcal{F}_{p,\tau,s}$, $1\le p\le 2$, we prove approximation bounds for shallow networks using spherical harmonic analysis. In particular, when the parameter measure is the uniform measure $\tau_{d}$ and $p* networks: the rates are $O\big(n^{-\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+d+1}}\log n\big)$ over $\mathscr{B}_{s}$ and $O\big(n^{-\frac{2\alpha}{2\alpha+d}}\log n\big)$ over $W^{\alpha,\infty}$, with matching lower bounds up to logarithmic factors.
Keywords: Neural Networks, Sobolev Spaces, Variation Spaces, Path Norm
1 Introduction
Deep learning has shown remarkable effectiveness in high-dimensional approximation problems, particularly in scientific computing, inverse problems, and operator learning (Han et al., 2018; Adcock et al., 2022; Beck et al., 2023). In many such settings, the ReLU* activation $\sigma_{s}(t)=\max\{0,t\}^{s}$ ($s\in\mathbb{N}_{0}$) is especially relevant because it yields piecewise-polynomial representations that are well suited to smooth targets and derivative-sensitive tasks (Yang and Zhou, 2025; He et al., 2024).
