A Note on Second-Order Expected Maximum-Load Bounds for
Binary Linear Hashing
Nader H. Bshouty
Technion
Abstract
Let $S \subset \mathbb{F}_2^n$ have size $n = 2^\ell$, and let $h : \mathbb{F}_2^n \to \mathbb{F}_2^\ell$ be a uniformly random linear map. For $y \in \mathbb{F}_2^\ell$, write $\text{load}_h(y) := |h^{-1}(y) \cap S|$, and let $\text{M}(S,h) := \max_{y\in\mathbb{F}_2^\ell} \text{load}_h(y)$ be the maximum load. Jaber, Kumar and Zuckerman (STOC 2025) proved that the expected maximum load of $h$ on $S$ is at most $16 \log n / \log \log n$, matching the fully independent keys-into-bins scale up to constants. Their proof also gives the tail estimate
$$\Pr\left[\text{M}(S,h) \geq R \frac{\log n}{\log \log n}\right] \leq O\left(\frac{1}{R^2}\right).$$
We record a base optimization in their exponential-potential method showing that binary linear hashing nearly matches fully independent hashing also at the level of the second-order maximum-load scale. For every $R > 1$ satisfying $R e^{\ell - 1/R} \leq D \ln \ell$, where $D$ is an absolute constant, we prove
$$\Pr\left[\text{M}(S,h) \geq R \frac{\log n}{\log \log n}\right] \leq O\left(\frac{(\log \log n)^2}{R^2 (\log n)^{2-2/R}}\right).$$
Integrating this tail yields
$$\mathbb{E}[\text{M}(S,h)] \leq \left(1 + (1 + o(1)) \frac{\log \log \log n}{\log \log n}\right) \frac{\log n}{\log \log n}.$$
Thus binary linear hashing matches fully independent hashing in the leading term and matches the dominant second-order correction up to a $1 + o(1)$ factor.
We also prove, by an independent self-contained argument, a sharp tail bound for one prescribed bucket: for fixed $y \in \mathbb{F}_2^\ell$,
$$\Pr[\text{load}_h(y) > 2^a - 2] \leq \gamma^{-1} 2^{-a^2},$$
where $\gamma = \prod_{j \geq 1} (1 - 2^{-j})$. A subspace construction shows that this is asymptotically tight even in the leading constant as $a \to \infty$. However, this controls only a fixed bucket; a direct union bound over all buckets loses a factor $2^\ell$.
