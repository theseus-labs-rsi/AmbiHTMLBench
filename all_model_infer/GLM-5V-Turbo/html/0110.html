c. It is unclear whether pre-vaccine nivolumab contributed to vaccine-induced T cell responses:
  • Major pathologic responses were noted post-vaccination melanoma patients who did not initially respond to nivolumab.
→Refining the sequence for different agents in combination immunotherapy for best clinical outcome needs further study.
  • Alternative immunologic agents, such as anti-CTLA-4, that can preferentially expand naïve CD8+ T cells to increase the T cell pool for vaccine priming, might be better a choice for pre-vaccine immunotherapy.
