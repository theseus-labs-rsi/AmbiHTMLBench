Asking for help fosters student success

Asking for help when necessary is critical to success in the workplace, but newcomers often fear how they will be perceived when they request assistance.

Understanding factors that motivate or inhibit various groups of students from seeking help will inform how to design interventions that will foster student success.

Positive experiences encourage students to ask for help

Many students reported feeling more comfortable after a positive experience with an instructor who reached out to them, or expressed a clear passion for teaching.

Students reported developing strategies for asking questions.

Most commonly doing as much of the work as possible in advance in order to form specific questions.

try to do all the work beforehand so I can point out to him exactly what's going wrong, and why I'm not understanding

Seeking help should be framed as an academic competency

This need to be predicated that knowing when to ask for help and how to competently ask for valued life-long skills

If students perceive asking for help effectively as an engineering competency, women and other underrepresented groups may experience fewer fears of stigmatization when asking questions.
