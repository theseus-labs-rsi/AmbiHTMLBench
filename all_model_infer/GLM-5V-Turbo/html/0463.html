WALLS:
Foundation walls shall be built of 12" concrete blocks below grade as per Building department Code, well laid up plumb and even with joints of portland cement mortar. Interior and exterior of all foundation walls to receive 2 coats of portland cement coating. Masonry walls above to be 8" cement blocks carried to height as shown on plans and given a coat of cement on all exposed surfaces. Coat of pitch or tar waterproofing to be put on exterior walls below grade.
CHIMNEY:
Build chimney of stone and brick top out as shown. Leave smoke pipe opening in cellar and provide metal thimble for same. Build in 48" Covet throat and damper and ash dump with clean-out door in cellar. Terra cotta flue lining in all flues in chimney of sizes as indicated on plans. Draught in chimney to be tested while being constructed and guaranteed to draw. Detail of fireplace will be submitted by Architect.
CELLAR FLOORS:
Cellar floors shall be composed of 4" cinder concrete, mixed 1 part portland cement, 2 parts sand, and 5 parts cinders and topped off with 1" portland cement mixed 1 cement and 3 sand, laid smooth and even and pitched to trap pit.
DRAIN PIPES:
Lay 4" earthenware drain pipes under earth to carry water from leaders to dry wells 8' from house. These pipes shall be dependent of sewer or other drain pipes and shall have the joints well cemented especially on the underside of same. Put in floor drains in cellar at boiler and in garage and run to stone dry wells. Lay septic tank drain pipes in best approved manner under supervision of Architect. 4" earthenware drain tile to be laid around footings of cellar on 4" gravel and covered with 2" gravel.
COLUMNS:
Provide and set C. I. concrete filled columns 4" in diameter in cellar for support of wood girder. Columns shall be set immediately after the girder is in place, and shall rest on concrete footings 12" thick and 24" square.
CINDER CONCRETE:
In bathrooms prepare floor with cinder concrete 3" thick and to within 1½" of finished floor. Carpenter will cut in the wood sheathing.
LATHING AND PLASTERING:
Lath all walls, partitions, ceilings and stair and soffits through 1st and 2nd stories including garage, cellar, ceiling, attic, etc., with black herringbone rib lath, lapping joints, and scratch and brown coat and finish on 1st and 2nd floors with coat of white approved hydrated lime, gauged with plaster of Paris all plumb, even and carefully trowelled to a smooth finish. Metal corner beads to be used on all angles and arched openings.
GRADING:
Contractor shall grade premises and lay down 4" rolled cinder driveway to garage and finish same with 3" bluestone. 3'0" wide flagstone walk from driveway to front steps. Terrace to receive 4" cinder concrete well tamped and finished with colored flagstone well cemented down.
SEEDING:
Contractor to seed entire lot at such time as directed by owner.
TIMBER:
All timber shall be of sound, full dimensioned spruce or fir, free from defects, large or loose knots affecting its durability and strength.
FRAMING:
Set all timbers in the usual manner to construct the building in accordance with the design. Frame to be balloon type. The studs shall extend from sill to plate and ribbon strip or girth shall be notched into studs at proper height to receive the floor beams. Sills shall be halved at corners and shall be embedded in cement on the wall. Ends of floor beams shall be placed against the studs wherever practical and spiked thereto. Floor beams shall be doubled under all partitions running the same way and also for headers and trimmers and stairwell, chimney and other openings. The tail beams and headers shall rest on a 2" x 3" strip or cleat spiked to the header and trimmer, and the tail beams shall be cut over and well spiked to the header. Spiking ends of tail beams to headers and headers to trimmers will not be permitted.
SIZES:
Sills 4" x 6", corner posts and intermediate posts, 4" x 6", ribbon strips or girts 1¼" x 6", plate 4" x 4", first tier 2" x 16"—16' c.c., 2nd and attic tier of beams, 2" x 8"—20' c.c., and 12' at long span; rafters 2" x 6"—20' c.c., studding 2" x 4"—16" c.c., braces 2" x 4", ridge 2" x 8"; girder in cellar 6" x 8" yellow pine, hip rafters 2" x 8".
BRIDGING:
Beams of all tiers shall be bridged with 1¼" x 3" herringbone bridging double nailed at each end. Bridging shall be cut in between beams before the underflooring is laid.
STUDDING:
Studding shall be double at each window, doors, and other openings, bearing partitions shall in all cases have two 2 x 4 sills and plates. Wherever practical the studs shall extend through the height of the floor beams and rest on the partition plate or story below and also on sill and girder to insure less cracks from shrinkage. Wide openings shall have 2—2" x 8" heads, ends of which shall in all cases rest on top of studs and not nailed in between same; corners of all intersecting partitions shall in all cases be made solid. Each corner of frame shall be braced with 2" x 4" braces, which shall extend over no less than three studs unless impractical.
SHEATHING:
All exterior sides of building shall be enclosed with 1" x 10" N. C. boards dressed one side, shiplap, put on horizontally and well nailed at each bearing. Sheathing shall be covered either with a heavy building paper or simply tarpaper, which shall also be well lapped behind all door and window casings.
ROOFS:
Frame and construct roofs shaped according to drawings and rafters with 1' x 2" shingle lath well nailed and even. Shingle lath to be covered with Pennsylvania Slate or with 18" Weatherbest or Creodipt stained shingles laid 5½" to the weather. Color of shingles to be as selected by owner.
FRAMES & SASH:
All window sash shall be of stock size as marked on plans and shall be double hung. Frames to be Andersen. Sash to be 1¼". All sash to be hung with Samson Spot Sash Cord.
SCREENS:
Screens to be applied on all window and door openings. Porch to be screened in entrance door as located by owner. All screens to be No. 18 mesh bronze wire.
EXTERIOR:
Exterior of building to be sheathed and covered with black building paper. 18" Weatherbest of Creodipt shingles to be applied over exterior and laid in pattern as directed by owner. Color of shingles to be selected by owner.
GLASS:
All glass to be bedded in putty except where wood stops are provided shall be secured with zinc sprigs and back putted. All glass to be double thick American sheet glass, clear, straight, and free from blisters and imperfections. Upon completion and before acceptance by the Owner, Contractor shall clean all glass and paint stains, etc., and replace all broken and defective glass and leave his portion in good repair and order.
EXTERIOR TRIM:
All exterior trim, such as cornices, posts, facia, boards, etc., shall be of cypress. Underside of main cornice projections shall be ceiled with ⅝" T & G. fir. Wherever mouldings occur, they shall be of stock pattern.
DOOR FRAMES:
Exterior door frames shall be 1¼" outside casing and 2" sills.
FLOOR:
Lay ⅞" N. C. underflooring throughout the 1st and 2nd story and in attic, on which lay the ⅞" x 2½" straight grained white oak floor on 1st and 2nd floor. All finished flooring shall be T. & G. and B. N. At the intersection of floor and base provide and put down stock floor mould or quarter round. Lay building paper between rough and finished flooring. Cut in deafening between beams in bathrooms, set about 4" below finished floor. All finished floors on 1st and 2nd floors shall be neatly scraped and sandpapered. Scraping of floors shall be deferred until the very last and at such times when painter can follow up with the finishing of same. Four strip oak border on floors in living room, dining room and master bedroom. Linoleum on kitchen floor to be laid by contractor.
TRIM AND BASE:
Trim throughout the house shall be 7⁄8" x 4" white pine, plain sills and aprons at windows; at doors trim shall extend to floor with base blocks. All trim in living room, dining room, library, and hall to be chestnut. Base shall be of white pine 6" high with 1½" moulding to suit. Closet base 7" high without moulding. Window and door openings shall be ½" x 1½" stock moulded stop heads. Built in ironing board in kitchen. Bookcases, dining room cabinet and living room panelled wainscoting to be as shown on drawing supplied by Architect.
WEATHER STRIPPING:
All exterior doors and windows to be weather-stripped. Front door to have 4" bronze saddle.
INSULATION:
All exterior walls and attic floor beams to be insulated with 4" mineral wool hand packed in a compact manner leaving no air spaces or voids between packing.
JAMBS AND HEADS:
Jambs and heads for all interior doors shall be of ⅞" white pine as required.
DOORS:
Doors shall be of sizes on drawings and shall be one panelled white pine, "Klimax" or Birch veneer doors. In rooms where chestnut trim might be used, doors to be of similar material. Front door shall be of 2½" oak and other exterior doors 1¾" of cypress or white pine. Include 1 panel door about 18" x 24" for bathroom waste valve. Door openings throughout to have moulded saddles of oak same as floors.
CLOSETS:
All closets shall have on 12" shelf, strip for hooks. Linen closet, however, shall have five 12" shelves. Any special closets shall be built as per detail by architect or "Morgan Millwork plate number.
STAIRS:
Cellar stairs shall be 1½" strings, 1¼" x 9" treads and necessary handrails, all of spruce. Main stairs shall have 1¼" wall and string planks, 1¼" treads 7⁄8" risers with moulded nosing, newels, platforms, etc., all of chestnut with red oak treads and platforms, except hand rail, which shall be of wrought iron. Stairs shall be properly housed, wedged and glued, provide a 1" well-hole facia, mouldings, etc., and necessary carriage timbers and furring for lathing. The stair contractor shall take measurements of well-hole and head room and shall submit layout of all staircases to Architect before building same.
HARDWARE:
The carpenter shall provide all hardware, such as nails, screws, cellar, window hardware, etc., and shall also include furnishing and application of finished hardware of hammered metal type. Hardware to be selected by owner. Allowance for same to be One Hundred Twenty-five ($125.00) Dollars.
DRESSERS:
Dressers in Kitchen and pantry shall be of wood with swinging sash doors, shelves, drawers and panelled cupboard doors, plate strip and shall extends to ceiling.
MEDICINE CABINETS:
Provide and set in one iron white enameled medicine cabinet in each bathroom with two shelves and bevel edge mirror in door. Cabinet to be about 21" x 27" in size, of Venetian Medicine Cabinet type.
WINDOW SHUTTERS:
Window shutters to be 1¼" stock and built as per detail shown on plans.
CUTTING:
Do all necessary cutting for all trades and put up usual cleats for plumber.
RUBBISH:
The carpenter shall remove from the building all rubbish except that of the mason and leave the building broom clean.
SCAFFOLDING:
Carpenter shall provide all necessary scaffolding for mason.
PAINTING EXTERIOR:
All exterior woodwork shall have all knots killed with shellac and all holes puttied and shall be painted 3 coats of Atlantic White Lead and pure linseed oil, or two coats of stain and one coat of varnish.
INTERIOR:
All interior woodwork shall be stained with an oil stain, all nail holes puttied and then varnished two coats with an approved make of varnish or at option of Owner, interior woodwork to be painted three coats of lead and oil including a finished coat of enamel. All woodwork to be well sandpapered and cleaned before the first coat is applied. All inside walls to receive three coats of lead and oil. Bathrooms and kitchen walls to receive finished coat of enamel. Any textured plaster to be stained and glazed. Cellar walls white-washed.
FLASHING, GUTTERS & LEADERS:
Use 16 oz. copper throughout for all the flashings. Flash the sides of the chimney where it intersects the roof and counterflash, same to be sawtooth. Flash over all window heads, sides of dormers, junctions of porches, and extensions to house, etc. Gutters and leaders to be copper as above. Gutters to be ½" round 5" wide and securely held in place with the copper hangers placed about 3' apart.
Provide the necessary sleeves and collars to connect to the leaders. All gutters to have the proper pitch to leaders.
All leaders to be 3" round corrugated or square and are to be of copper as above. Provide copper wire baskets at all leader openings in the gutters.
TILING WORK:
Kitchen walls 4'6" above floor to be prepared with wire lath and scratched with cement plaster to receive 4" x 4" tile. Bathroom walls to be tiled 4'6" high and floors to receive one inch hexagonal or square ceramic tile with ornamental border. Sanitary cove and base to be used. Tile to be 4'6" above bathtubs. Hearths to receive 4" x 4" quarry tile of colors to be selected by the Owner. Tile in master bathroom and lavatory and kitchen to be colored and selected by Owner. Bathroom accessories to consist of the following and shall be built-in with the tile. 1 soap and grab dish over bathtub, 1 glass holder, 1 shelf and soap dish under medicine chest, 1 toilet paper holder, 1 tooth brush holder, 2 towel bars, 2 towel bars in kitchen.
All tile work to be neatly buttered and at completion washed with white cement and thoroughly cleaned of all foreign substance.
PLUMBING GENERAL:
The plumbing shall be done in the best manner complying with all requirements of the Board of Health. The plumber shall pay all fees and get all the necessary permits, also doing all excavations and refilling for the laying of pipes. Cast iron pipes and fittings, etc., which are underground shall be of extra heavy cast iron. House sewer shall be earthenware within 2' of the house wall and house drain, trap, fresh air inlet, and soil shall be of cast iron and vent and waste line of standard galvanized iron and all short branches to waste and soil and soil lines shall be of lead. Pure pig lead shall be used for caulking, and connections between lead and lead shall be by wiped joints and between lead and iron by brass ferrules and caulking. All horizontal runs shall have no less fall than ¼" per foot. Use Y and 45 degree elbows for changes of direction and all vertical lines by 90 degree Y, all properly fastened and supported.
SEPTIC TANK:
The plumber shall make proper connection with septic tank by means of a 4" earthenware pipe, which shall be continued to within two feet of the house line where it shall be connected with the cast iron house drain. Septic tank shall be 900 gallon capacity built of concrete as manufactured by the Nustone Products Corporation.
HOUSE DRAIN, ETC.:
House drain shall be 4" cast iron, screw cap and trap placed in cellar. Trap shall have a 4" cast iron, fresh air inlet, place in house side of trap and carried to outside with a cast iron perforated plate in wall. Drains in cellar to be piped to blind drain.
