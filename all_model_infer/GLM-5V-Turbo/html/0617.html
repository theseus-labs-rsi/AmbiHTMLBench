记$\triangle ABC$的内角$A,B,C$的对边分别为$a,b,c$.已知$\displaystyle\frac{\cos A}{1+\sin A}=\frac{\sin 2B}{1+\cos 2B}$.
(1)若$C=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$,求$B$;
(2)求$\dfrac{a^{3}+b^{3}}{c^{3}}$的最小值.
【解析】$\displaystyle\frac{\sin 2B}{1+\cos 2B}=\frac{2\sin B\cos B}{2\cos ^{2}B}=\frac{\sin B}{\cos B}=\frac{\cos A}{1+\sin A}$
$\Rightarrow \sin B+\sin B\sin A=\cos A\cos B$
$\Rightarrow \sin B=\cos(B+A)=\sin(B+A)=\cos(B+A)=-\cos C$
$\because B,C\in(0,\pi),\therefore\sin B>0\Rightarrow\cos C<0,C>\dfrac{\pi}{2},B\in(0,\dfrac{\pi}{2})$,
$\sin B=\sin(C-\dfrac{\pi}{2})\Rightarrow B=C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$或$B+C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\pi$(舍)

$(1)B=C-\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\dfrac{5\pi}{6}-\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$

$(2)$因为我们有角的关系,我们将边化为角:$A=\pi-B-C=\pi-2B,C=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+B$
由正弦定理:$a^{3}+b^{3}:\sin ^{3}A+\sin ^{3}B=\dfrac{\cos ^{3}2B+\sin ^{3}B}{\sin ^{3}C}=\dfrac{(2\cos ^{2}B-1)^{3}+1-\cos ^{3}B}{\cos ^{3}B}$
令$x=\cos B,x\in(0,1)$
$\dfrac{a^{3}+b^{3}}{c^{3}}=\dfrac{(2x^{2}-1)^{3}+1-x^{3}}{x^{3}}=\dfrac{8x^{6}-12x^{4}+6x^{2}-x^{3}}{x^{3}}=4x^{3}-\dfrac{5}{x}+2\geqslant 2\sqrt[4]{5}-5=4\sqrt[4]{2}-5$
$4x=2\sqrt[4]{2}\Rightarrow x=\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}$时取等号

如图直三棱柱$ABC-A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$的体积为$4,\triangle A_{1}BC$的面积为$2\sqrt{2}$.
(1)求$A_{1}$到平面$ABC$的距离;
(2)设$D$为$A_{1}C$的中点,$AA_{1}=AB$,平面$A_{1}BC\perp$平面$ABB_{1}A_{1}$,求二面角$A-BD-C$的正值.
【解答】$(1)$等体积法:设$A_{1}$到平面$ABC$的距离为$h$
$V_{A_{1}-ABC}=V_{A-A_{1}BC}\Rightarrow\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 4=\dfrac{1}{3}S_{\triangle A_{1}BC}\cdot h=h=\sqrt{2}$

$(2)$大家应该都能看出$BC\perp$平面$ABB_{1}A_{1}$,但就是很多人没证出来.
建系的话明显是容易取上.取$AB$中点$M$,过$MD$
$AA_{1}=AB,AA_{1}\perp AB\Rightarrow AM\perp AB,A_{1}B\perp$平面$A_{1}BC\perp$平面$ABB_{1}A_{1}$,则$AM\perp$平面$A_{1}BC,BC\subset$平面$A_{1}BC\Rightarrow AM\perp BC$,又$AA_{1}\perp BC\Rightarrow BC\perp$平面$ABB_{1}A_{1}$(省略了一点步骤,大家考场作答不要漏).
作$MN\perp BD$,连$AN$,易证$\angle MNA$即为$A_{1}-BD-A$的二面角,也是二面角$A-BD-C$的补角,两角正弦值相等.
图中各关系易得,$BD=\sqrt{3}$,等面积法求得$MN=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3},AN=\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$
故$\sin\angle MNA=\dfrac{AM}{AN}=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
